# Private executive briefing, session-only

URL: /executive-private-room/

The form operates inside the open page. It has no localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies created by the briefing app, sharing URLs, backend, third-party prompt calls, hidden form submission, background autosave, or disclosure of field contents in analytics events. It uses DOM textContent when rendering supplied text, never unescaped innerHTML.

The visitor controls Export JSON and Print/Save PDF. These actions produce local artifacts controlled by the visitor and are not encrypted. Clearing the session removes form content from the current page only; downloaded and printed copies remain under visitor control. Browser crash recovery and collaboration are not claimed.

The general site consent-aware analytics loader remains present for site-wide compatibility. An opted-in analytics service might record the page view, never the briefing data. This is not a multi-user authenticated secure room. For restricted board materials use an approved private service, not a public site.

Audience prompts and professional descriptions remain first-party statements; do not invent references, employer-issued employment proofs or deal metrics.
