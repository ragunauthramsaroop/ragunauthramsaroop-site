SSRN Submission Assistant

This review workspace organizes research manuscripts and verified metadata before depositing them through the official SSRN form. It does not log in to SSRN, send papers automatically, bypass access controls, or describe a paper as published before SSRN confirms it.

Author approval required. SSRN requires genuine copyright and research-integrity declarations, including an accurate statement of the specific AI tools and their contribution. Confirm all information yourself before you click Submit on SSRN. Submitting many papers at once may prompt additional scrutiny.

Submission queue

Get prepared PDF ZIP from GitHub Actions Open my SSRN papers

In GitHub Actions, choose the newest successful SSRN Publication Bridge audit run and download the artifact named ssrn-author-upload-package. Inside it, use the ZIP containing reviewed-file candidates and submission-metadata.json. The prepared PDFs contain the required disclosure appendix; original PDFs in the repository do not replace them.

Submission sequence

  1. Download the prepared PDF ZIP and review the paper, funding declarations, author's affiliations, AI tool names, copyright permission and every cited source.
  2. Sign in through the official SSRN page. Search My Papers to prevent duplicates and preserve existing abstract IDs.
  3. Choose New Submission. Upload one matching -SSRN-author-review.pdf from the ZIP.
  4. Copy the title, writing date, abstract, keywords and suggested classification using the controls above. Include the accurate AI disclosure in both the PDF and submission details.
  5. Confirm author identity, classification, research integrity, licence choice and author-rights declarations. Read the final SSRN preview before submitting.
  6. After SSRN confirms acceptance of the form, record its actual submission ID. Public posting, DOI assignment and Google Scholar indexing are separate states.
