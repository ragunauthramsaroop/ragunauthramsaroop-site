STANDARD · ~150 WORDS

Ragunauth "Randy" Ramsaroop is a Guyanese executive working across corporate affairs, ESG, government relations, governance and compliance. His career spans regulated financial services, digital strategy and large-scale mining. Since joining AGM Inc. in 2020, his responsibilities have expanded across government and regulatory engagement, corporate coordination, ESG and social responsibility. His current confirmed role is Liaison Director, Social Responsibility Department. His public record includes international management development, the 2021 Best of Best Second Merit Award, the 2023 Management Reserve Program, and a Climate Leadership Research Centre profile hosted on the University of Oxford domain. The official UNGIS-MCG directory also lists him as a Private Sector member. He publishes independent research on responsible mining, governance, energy transition and institutional trust.
