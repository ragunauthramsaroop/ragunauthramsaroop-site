Executive findings

What the ranking hides

Mixed fiscal windows materially weaken any simple league table. Microsoft closes in June 2026, NVIDIA in January 2026, Apple in September 2025, while most others close in December 2025.

Berkshire’s GAAP net earnings require a separate operating bridge because investment gains and impairments materially affect the headline.

JPMorgan belongs in a bank-specific lane using net interest income, credit costs, CET1 and ROTCE rather than industrial margin or conventional free cash flow.

Aramco’s infographic figure aligns to shareholder-attributable profit, not total group profit. The ownership basis must stay visible.

Board question: which earnings streams retain their economics after period normalization, capital-intensity adjustment and removal of one-off effects?
