01

Foundation in regulated service

The career began in financial services, where accuracy, client trust, controls and consistency were daily disciplines. Digital-strategy work then added client communication, commercial thinking and the ability to translate between creative, technical and business priorities.

02

AGM leadership tenure, consolidated from 2020

AGM tenure is presented as one consolidated record from 2020 to the present. Earlier internal titles are intentionally omitted unless reconfirmed. The current confirmed role is Liaison Director, Social Responsibility Department, with scope across ESG, government and regulatory engagement, corporate coordination, stakeholder strategy, social performance, executive communication and cross-functional delivery.

03

Management development

Professional development includes selection for Zijin Mining Group's First Non-Chinese Employee Training at headquarters in March–April 2023 and selection for the Management Reserve Program in 2023, adding group-level exposure to culture, management expectations and international operations.

04

International professional participation

In 2026, the United Nations Group on the Information Society listed Ragunauth as a Private Sector member of its Multistakeholder Consultation Group, with his AGM Inc. role and Guyana affiliation. The official directory verifies consultation-group membership, not United Nations employment or a diplomatic appointment.

05

Leadership approach

The leadership approach is evidence-led, institutionally aware and execution-focused. It begins with the business objective, regulatory requirement and stakeholder expectation, then establishes ownership, documents commitments, escalates material issues and keeps execution aligned with governance, safety and operating reality.
