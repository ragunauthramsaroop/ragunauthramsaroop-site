International keynote & strategic dialogue · 19 November 2026

When mining builds a nation.

The link between investment, government and territorial development in Guyana.

Ragunauth Ramsaroop · Liaison Director, Social Responsibility Department, AGM Inc. · Panama City, Panama

The central message.

At Expominera Internacional 2026, I shared Guyana's experience of natural-resource development and its relationship with investment, government institutions and local communities. My keynote examined a responsibility extending beyond the production and commercial performance of a mine: ensuring economic activity contributes to stronger institutions, expanded opportunity and enduring national capability.

Responsible mining depends on the quality of partnerships between government, industry, communities and local businesses. Leadership requires clear governance, credible commitments and the discipline to translate investment into outcomes people experience.

Keynote address · 19 November 2026

Investment and national development.

When mining builds a nation: the link between investment, government and territorial development in Guyana.

The address considered how responsible investment, institutional coordination, workforce development, environmental stewardship and domestic participation together shape mining's contribution to Guyana's long-term progress.

Strategic dialogue · 19 November 2026

From commitment to shared prosperity.

Shared prosperity: from commitment to the transformation of territories.

I joined fellow panellists Viviana Santinón Alonso, Aurelia Tot Maas and Silvio Dal Buoni in a discussion moderated by Cheryl Watemberg Velásquez. The exchange focused on converting social-investment commitments into meaningful regional and community outcomes.

The legacy of responsible mining is measured by the opportunities created, the lives improved and the strength of the institutions left for future generations.
