DECISION DISCIPLINE

How to interpret the signals

01
Trend is not value

A short-term sell indication reflects recent price action, not a validated 2029 valuation.

02
Confirmation matters

Compare momentum shifts with volume, company announcements and gold price movements.

03
Time horizons differ

An employee award with 2029 vesting is not comparable with a short-term trading position.

04
Data latency matters

Delayed charts and indicative exchange-session status must never be mistaken for a live order book.

06 / AUDIT TRAIL

Data provenance & safeguards

Market-source separation and modelling assumptions, visible by design.

06
01

Market widgets

Yahoo Finance OHLC history is retrieved by a read-only Vercel API with symbol verification and provider quote timestamp. Data availability, delay and downstream usage rights remain subject to Yahoo and HKEX terms. Chart computation runs locally.

02

Historical references

Live-data-labelled KPIs use the newest available 2259.HK, 2899.HK and GC=F provider observations with timestamps. Historical published mining targets and incentive-plan prices are labelled reference assumptions, never live data.

03

Scenario modelling

2027–2029 prices recalculate from validated delayed stock and gold feeds plus your editable assumptions. Market-linked models remain hypothetical sensitivity outputs, not analyst consensus or guaranteed returns.

04

Privacy

No account, cookie, tracker or browser storage is used by the award calculator. Personal share counts never leave the browser. Separate read-only market-data requests retrieve only fixed public symbols.
