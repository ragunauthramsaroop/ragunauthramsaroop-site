## Audit opinion

**VISUAL QA PASS, CONDITIONAL INVESTMENT RESEARCH SYSTEM.** The current review branch passes all seven tested viewport geometry checks, 10 mock-fed browser integration checks and 34 dedicated source and quantitative tests. Mobile and desktop Lighthouse workflows passed their configured gates. These results do not establish investment-grade forecast accuracy, full accessibility conformance or exchange-data licensing.

**Market-data entitlement:** No licensed real-time HKEX API credentials were configured in the inspected Vercel project. The public market chart API uses unofficial, delayed Yahoo Finance market history. The separate direct licensed quote endpoint correctly fails closed with HTTP 503. Authorised free Basic Market Price snapshots are available on the licensed etnet provider's own site only, with user-initiated retrieval.

## Financial and documentary verification

1. **FY2025 audited mined gold output**: 46.9 tonnes across the nine producing operations. The source table reconciles the project breakdown and prior-year comparison. Source: [ZGI Annual Report 2025, Table 12](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0429/2026042902638.pdf).
2. **FY2025 group AISC**: US$1,501/oz. Source: FY2025 Annual Report.
3. **January-September 2026 unaudited mined gold**: approximately 41 tonnes; **AISC US$1,649/oz**. Source: [Nine-Month 2026 operational announcement](https://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1019/2026101900165.pdf).
4. **2026 mined gold production target**: 59.2 tonnes; **2028 guidance**: 70-75 tonnes. These are corporate targets, not realised output.
5. The 2027-2029 valuation grid is a sensitivity tool. It does not represent official sell-side analyst targets, a mine-level discounted cash-flow model, calibrated market probabilities, or investment advice.
6. The conditional share-award screen is locally computed and does not authorise vesting. It relies on individual input and published company scheme terms.
