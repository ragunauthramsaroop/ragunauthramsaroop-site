Executive profile

Leadership where enterprise strategy meets country execution.

Director-level executive with 12+ years across multinational mining, regulated financial services and commercial operations. Currently Liaison Director, Social Responsibility Department at AGM Inc., part of Zijin Gold International.

Career scope spans government and regulatory relations, ESG, social performance, corporate affairs, compliance coordination, executive communications and strategic stakeholder engagement. The leadership approach converts external expectations, policy signals and stakeholder priorities into internal ownership, evidence requirements, decision paths and accountable follow-through.

Best suited to country, regional and international mandates requiring trusted institutional relationships, disciplined execution and senior-level coordination across government, communities, regulators and operating teams.
